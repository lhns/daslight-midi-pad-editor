OUT/OUT1/OUT2 String:
AAA:B:CC:D:MINIIN2

Meaning:
AAA: indicated the button (section of the button) - should not be changed!
B: LED flashing-mode (0 on, 1 flashing)
CC: indicated the button (exact button), where the second letter indicates the column - should not be changed!
D: LED color-code

Explanations:
OUT is the LED button-status when Off
OUT1 is the LED status when the button-status is On
OUT2 is the LED status when the button-status is Transitioning

------------

Rules
- Button LEDs on every Column in the list should be identical, following the button-color-mapping.
- LED color-code should be always the same for button-status Off, On and Transitioning
- LED flashing-mode should be on for button-status Off; LED flashing-mode should be flashing for button-status On and Transitioning
